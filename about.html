Our Story & Philosophy

Engineering homes that respond quietly, beautifully, and reliably.

Founded in Guwahati, Nexoraa was established to address a persistent challenge in Northeast India: smart home installations were frequently fragmented, unreliable, and locked into rigid single-brand proprietary ecosystems. We set out to pioneer an architectural engineering approach to home automation.

Architectural Engineering

We treat home automation as an essential architectural discipline. Structured cabling, thermal dissipation, surge suppression, power isolation, and local computing servers are planned long before finishing work begins.

Brand-Neutral Freedom

We represent no single equipment vendor. We evaluate and integrate the finest global hardware based strictly on long-term build quality, local repairability, open standard protocols, and seamless interoperability.

Local Dedicated Support

Our full engineering and technical support team is rooted right here in Guwahati. When you require assistance or want to refine an automation routine, you deal directly with our local engineers.

Our Methodology

Craftsmanship meets technical rigor

From initial floor plan blue-printing to post-handover white-glove support, we follow a rigorous 4-stage engineering lifecycle.

Phase 01

Architectural Consultation & CAD Design

We collaborate directly with your architect, interior designer, and electrical consultants to specify cable pathways, conduit schedules, equipment rack spaces, and keypads prior to construction.

Phase 02

Structured Wiring & Rack Fabrication

All low-voltage wiring (Cat6A, optical fiber, high-grade speaker cable) is pulled, labeled, certified, and terminated into thermal-managed centralized equipment racks.

Phase 03

Hardware Commissioning & Programming

We configure local hub servers, lighting dimmers, DALI-2 drivers, audio zones, security cameras, and access keypads, ensuring all routines execute locally with sub-second latency.

Phase 04

White-Glove Handover & Local Support

We train clients and household staff on operating the system, provide custom documentation, and back every project with dedicated local support.
